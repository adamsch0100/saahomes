The numbers most pricing pages show are national averages. This page shows something narrower and more useful: what happened, listing by listing, in a single competitive set around one home. The source is the ListLogic Market Fingerprint for 1843 24th Ave Ct in West Greeley. It tracks a set of nearby homes as they list, go under contract and sell.

The subject home went on the market June 4, 2026 with an initial asking price of $410,000. The Fingerprint was current as of July 23, 2026. Its original competitive set held 26 homes; 6 were still active and 20 were pending or sold. The figures below were verified 2026-09-30.

How to read these numbers

The six figures count different events in the same set, so they do not sum to one total. A home can be listed, go under contract and sell, and it is counted in each of those. The 5 under and 12 over are the split of the 17 new listings by how they were priced against the subject home. That is why 17 + 5 + 12 + 13 + 14 is larger than 44.

This is one competitive set around one listing in West Greeley. It is a worked example of how list price sits against odds and supply, not a market average and not a price for any single home.

Why a listing agent would show this

A seller can argue with one number. A snapshot of what the competing homes actually did is harder to argue with. The 5 under and 12 over split shows a seller where the other new listings chose to price, and the 13 under contract against 14 sold shows how many of them found a buyer. Put that beside the home's own price and the question changes from “what is it worth” to “which price gets me a buyer.”

FAQ

How many homes in this market file were priced under and over the subject home?

Of the 17 new listings that came on in the competitive set, 5 were priced under the subject home and 12 were priced over it. The other figures measure different events in the same set, so they do not add up to the 44 homes that moved.

What is a Market Fingerprint?

A Market Fingerprint is ListLogic's weekly seller picture for one listing. It shows what went pending around the home, what listed under or over it, and which nearby homes look like the better buy as the market changes.

Do these numbers apply to another market?

No. They come from one West Greeley competitive set tied to one listing, 1843 24th Ave Ct, and were verified 2026-09-30. Treat them as a worked example, not a statewide or national statistic.

Colorado housing market · August 2026

Colorado's August 2026 market tipped further toward buyers. Closed sales fell across the state and homes took longer to sell, while the statewide median price held steady and active inventory dropped. Buyers had more room to negotiate, and sellers who priced to the current set were the ones who moved.

How to use these numbers in a listing appointment

A statewide number is context, not a price. The seller's question is what their home will sell for against the homes a buyer would cross-shop. Put the two together: 4.8 months of supply and 65 days to sell tell a seller the market has choice and time, and the local competitive set tells them which choice a buyer makes. FAQ

How much housing supply does Colorado have in August 2026?

Colorado carried 4.8 months' supply of inventory statewide in August 2026, down from 5.1 months in July, according to the Colorado Association of REALTORS® August 2026 market trends report released September 15, 2026. Active inventory fell 6.2% year over year to 34,488 properties as buyer demand softened.

Are Colorado home prices falling in 2026?

The statewide median sale price held steady at $550,000 across all property types in August 2026, per the Colorado Association of REALTORS®. Closed sales fell 11.3% year over year and homes took an average of 65 days to sell, up 8.3%, so sellers faced longer marketing times even as the median price held.

Where do these Colorado numbers come from?

Every figure on this page comes from the Colorado Association of REALTORS® Market Trends Housing Report for August 2026, released September 15, 2026, and links to that release. The figures were verified 2026-10-02.

Questions about the numbers

What were U.S. existing home sales in August 2026?

Existing homes sold at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 3.98 million in August 2026, down 2.0% from July and 1.2% from a year earlier — the first month below 4.0 million since June 2025. Source: National Association of Realtors newsroom, released September 10, 2026.

What was the median existing-home price in August 2026?

The median existing-home price was $429,100 in August 2026, up 1.6% year over year — the 38th straight month of year-over-year gains. Source: National Association of Realtors.

How much housing supply was there in August 2026?

Existing-home supply was 4.9 months in August 2026, up from 4.6 months in July and the highest in over ten years, with 1.62 million existing homes for sale at the end of the month. New-home supply stood at 8.5 months at the August sales pace, down from 9.0 in July. Sources: National Association of Realtors; U.S. Census Bureau and FRED series MSACSR.

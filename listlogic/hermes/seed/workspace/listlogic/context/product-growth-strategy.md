# Product growth strategy — ListLogic

## North star
Paid activations: Stripe trials → subscriptions, one-shots, brokerage seats.

## ICP
Listing-active agents (≈6+ seller appointments/year), listing teams, brokerages with listing culture. Nationwide.

## Wedge
Soft market / sellers overprice → custom-fit market + live price×odds×supply story.

## Funnel (locked)
Demo free → signup/setup free → Generate teaser wall → 7-day card trial ($39/mo after) or $20 one-shot.

## Channels
1. Adam CB / warm personal (text+email)
2. Hermes-built nationwide prospect CRM → email (CAN-SPAM) + personal SMS packs
3. Consented SMS scale later (10DLC)
4. SEO on homepage + comparison pages
5. Brokerage pilots
6. MLS vendor research (slow)

## Competitors
Cloud CMA (MLS bundle), Saleswise ($39/7-day), RPR free research, CMAGPT/CMAForge AI. Win on interactive pricing story + any-MLS upload + Search — not as “another CMA PDF.”

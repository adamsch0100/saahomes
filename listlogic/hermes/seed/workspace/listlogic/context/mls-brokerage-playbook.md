# MLS & brokerage partnership playbook

## Principle
**Never pause direct agent sales waiting on MLS.** Parallel track: agents pay today via Search/Upload; MLS deals multiply distribution later.

## Integration ladder (realistic)
| Stage | What we offer | What we need | When |
|-------|---------------|--------------|------|
| 0 Now | Any-MLS CSV/TXT upload + nationwide Search | Nothing | Live |
| 1 Pilot | Branded office seats + trainer kit | Brokerage champion | Now |
| 2 Association / MLS “preferred vendor” | Member discount codes, webinar slot, landing on MLS app store | Vendor app, insurance, SOC/security Qs, RESO interest | 30–90d research |
| 3 Data feed | RESO Web API / licensed feed into Search | Contract + compliance | Only after Stage 2 traction |
| 4 Deep embed | SSO / one-click from MLS dashboard | Engineering sprint + MLS IT | Last |

Do **not** promise live MLS sync in marketing until Stage 3 is signed.

## MLS / association pitch (elevator)
Members lose listings to overpricing and portal anchors. Bundled CMAs produce reports; they don’t force the **price vs odds vs new supply** conversation. ListLogic is the seller pricing story layer — demo free for members, unlock at Generate. Pilot N offices; measure days-on-market and reduction events.

## Brokerage pitch (elevator)
Make listing appointments consistent across the office. Shared branding, seat pricing from $29/seat, lunch-and-learn in 30 minutes. Agents keep using whatever MLS they have today (upload or Search). You get fewer stale listings and a modern wow without ripping out your CRM stack.

4. Team leads with public listing volume

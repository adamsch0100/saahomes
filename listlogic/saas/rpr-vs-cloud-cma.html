RPR vs Cloud CMA

RPR vs Cloud CMA: the honest comparison for listing agents

Short version: RPR is deep research that ships with your NAR membership. Cloud CMA is the polished, live-MLS report — often already paid for by your MLS. Here's what each one actually does in a listing appointment, and the one question both leave you to answer alone.

Both answer "what did homes sell for?"

Neither one shows a seller what happens if you stretch the price. That question — price × odds × supply — is where listing appointments in a soft market are actually won or lost, and it's the gap this comparison is really about.

What each tool actually is

Based on publicly available product information — both are good tools, and they solve different problems.

RPR

Research depth, at no extra cost

RPR (REALTORS® Property Resource) is included with NAR membership at no additional cost and is available only to REALTORS®. It layers MLS data with public records, tax history, and market analytics, and produces reports — including a seller-ready Seller's Report. Its 2026 AI-assisted CMA scores comps for relevance and lets you tighten the criteria before the pull. If you haven't logged in lately, it's better than its old reputation.

Cloud CMA

The category standard for the packet

Cloud CMA (Lone Wolf) pulls live MLS data into branded CMA reports, buyer tours, and seller net sheets. Its live presentation mode turns the report into something you can run on screen with the seller following along — which is why it became the default. You pick the comps yourself; the tool makes them look professional.

What RPR costs

$0 beyond your dues

RPR is included with NAR membership at no additional cost. The real price is workflow: the output is data-dense and agent-side, so most agents reformat it before a seller ever sees it.

What Cloud CMA costs

Check your MLS first

Lone Wolf doesn't publish a price on the product page. Third-party software listings put Cloud CMA at roughly $49/month or $499/year. But many MLSs buy it association-wide and hand it to members at no charge — so the honest first step is checking your MLS, and only then deciding whether the presentation mode is worth the subscription.

RPR vs Cloud CMA: how to actually choose

If your MLS includes Cloud CMA

Keep it. Seriously.

Free through your MLS plus free-with-dues RPR covers the pricing analysis for $0. There's no reason to pay for a replacement that does the same job. Spend the money only where the appointment is actually failing.

If you're paying for Cloud CMA

Let volume decide

If you build reports every week, the subscription is a rounding error against the listings it supports. If you build a handful of CMAs a year, a fixed monthly line item between uses is worth re-checking — including whether your MLS would hand it to you for free.

If you lose on price, not on comps

The packet isn't the problem

When a seller is anchored on a portal estimate, a more polished report still reads as an argument. The conversation changes when they see their own odds drop as the price climbs. Neither RPR nor Cloud CMA shows that — comps are history, and the seller is deciding on the future.

The stack most agents land on

Research anywhere. Decide together.

There's no loyalty oath. Research in RPR or Cloud CMA — then bring ListLogic to the table for the pricing decision: a market customized to the subject home, live what-if odds, print, flipbook, and a seller link that stays current after they list.

Where both leave you exposed in a soft market

In a soft market the seller's anchor is a portal number, inventory is stacking, and every buyer is cross-shopping price, condition, and location. Comps tell you what already sold. They can't answer the three questions that decide the listing:

The question

What happens at each price?

ListLogic prices against the home's own competitive set — not city-wide averages — and shows, at every list price, the odds of selling in a realistic window and the supply pressure behind those odds. The stretch price stops being an opinion and becomes a visible trade-off.

The outcome

The seller chooses with you

Overpricing gets discussed while you're in the room instead of after 30 days on market. The seller walks away with the story: interactive Live Story, print listing presentation, listing flipbook, and a living link that shows what listed under and over them. That's the appointment RPR and Cloud CMA were never built to run.

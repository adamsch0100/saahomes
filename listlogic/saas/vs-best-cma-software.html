What to actually look for in 2026

1 · Competitive set

Custom-fit, not city-wide

The best CMA builds a competitive set for the specific home — size, bedrooms, garage, age, condition, area. Averages hide the homes a buyer will actually cross-shop against.

2 · The pricing conversation

Can you show the trade-off?

When a seller is anchored on Zillow, a comp PDF doesn't move them. Showing what happens to their odds at every stretch price does. That's the difference between a report and a decision tool.

3 · Your MLS, your way

No vendor lock-in

You should be able to use your own MLS data — search any market or upload any export. If a tool forces you into one data pipeline, you're paying for their integration, not your workflow.

4 · The leave-behind

Print + flipbook

The pricing story should travel: print it, send it as a flipbook, leave it on the seller's fridge. The listing is won after you leave too.

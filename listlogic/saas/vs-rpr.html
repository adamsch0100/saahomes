The real difference: data tools vs. decision tools

Research

RPR is where you research

Comps, property history, analytics, tax data — RPR is a serious agent-side tool. If you're a REALTOR®, use it. The problem isn't the data; it's that a seller can't follow a dense report in an appointment.

Presentation

ListLogic is where you present

One story: here's your competitive set, here's what sells, and here's what happens to your odds at every price. Interactive, printable, sendable. Built for the person across the table, not the person at the desk.

The anchor

Data doesn't change minds. Story does.

A seller anchored at Zillow's estimate isn't moved by more comps — they're moved when they see their own odds drop as the price climbs. That's a decision tool, and it's what wins the listing.

The stack

Use both

There's no loyalty oath. Research in RPR, present in ListLogic. The only thing you're missing by not trying ListLogic is the one slide that closes the appointment.

Fair questions, honest answers

Is ListLogic a replacement for RPR?

Not necessarily. RPR is excellent for research. ListLogic is the seller-facing pricing story — the part of your workflow the client actually experiences. Many agents run both: RPR for the deep dive, ListLogic for the appointment.

Does ListLogic have as much data as RPR?

RPR has an enormous national dataset. ListLogic uses Search on any market or your own MLS upload, which means the data is always relevant to the market you're presenting in — and you're never locked to one data vendor.

Will this help me win listings?

That's the point. ListLogic turns pricing from an argument into a shared reading of the market. Agents who show price × odds × supply have a different conversation — one where the seller sees the market's answer, not just your opinion.

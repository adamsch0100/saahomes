What to watch for in “free” CMA tools

Free comparative market analysis software for real estate agents is rarely free in the way the search suggests. Check the lock, the story, and the ads.

Data-lock

Bundled only while you stay

MLS-bundled report tools are “free” until you change boards. If the software leaves when the MLS does, you do not own the workflow. ListLogic: Search any market or upload any MLS export — you pay ListLogic, keep your MLS.

No odds

Comps without the trade-off

A free generator that prints solds still leaves you arguing the number. If it cannot show price × odds × supply, it is a report, not a pricing conversation. That is the appointment.

Ads and lead-gen

The product is your contact list

Some “free CMA” pages are consumer lead forms or ad-supported PDFs. Fine if you wanted a flyer. Not fine if you needed seller-ready proof with your name on it.

Wrong industry

Bare “free CMA” is polluted

Search “free CMA” and you will hit accounting, CAM/CNC, and medical results. Always qualify: free CMA software for realtors, or free CMA for real estate agents. This page is the real-estate tool — not those.

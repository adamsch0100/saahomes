Fair questions, honest answers

What is seller CMA software?

Seller CMA software helps real estate agents prepare a Comparative Market Analysis for a listing appointment. Most tools stop at comparable sales. ListLogic adds the pricing-decision layer: a custom-fit competitive set plus interactive price × odds × supply, so the seller sees the trade-off at every price before the sign goes up.

How is ListLogic different from standard seller CMA tools?

Standard seller CMA tools show comps in a report or slide deck. ListLogic is built around the pricing decision: for each possible list price, the seller sees their odds of selling and the supply pressure from homes a buyer would actually cross-shop. It turns a pricing dispute into a shared reading of the market.

Does ListLogic need a live MLS feed?

No. ListLogic works by Search on any market nationwide, or by uploading any MLS export — Matrix, RESO-style, and common CSV/TXT formats. You confirm the column mapping before generating. No live MLS integration or vendor contract required.

What does the seller actually see?

An interactive pricing story: their home's competitive set, the odds of selling at each price point, and the supply pressure from comparable homes — plus a print PDF and flipbook leave-behind of the same story.

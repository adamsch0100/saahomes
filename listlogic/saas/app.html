Homes in the file

Create your seller's custom market — the homes a buyer will actually cross-shop against theirs. Filter in MLS, export, then upload.

  1. Statuses: Active, Pending / Backup, Sold, Expired, and Withdrawn.
  2. Property type that matches the subject (house vs condo / townhome).
  3. Area a buyer would cross-shop (city, subdivision, or map).
  4. Time: last 2 years by list date or close date.
  5. Size band: living / total square footage around the subject.
  6. Beds, baths, and garage spaces in a tight range around the subject.
  7. Lot size (acres or sq ft) when lot matters for this home.
  8. Export as CSV or TXT — aim for about 500 homes. If your MLS only offers Excel, Save As CSV.
Then in ListLogic: upload the file. We auto-match headers (MLS #, status, price, sold price, beds, baths, sq ft, garage, dates, address, lot, and more). Check the dropdowns — change any that are wrong — then Confirm mapping and Generate.

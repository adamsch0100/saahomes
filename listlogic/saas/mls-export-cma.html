How to create a CMA from an MLS export (Matrix, Flexmls, Bright)

These steps work with the export you already know how to pull. No live feed, no integration queue, no waiting on your MLS to approve anything.

  • Export comps from your MLS

    Run the comp search you'd normally use for a CMA in Matrix, Flexmls, Bright, or any system with a CSV/TXT export.

  • Upload the file (or Search instead)

    Drag the CSV/TXT into ListLogic. No file handy? Search the market by address and filters instead.

  • Confirm the column mapping

    ListLogic proposes how Beds, Sq Ft, MLS #, and Close Price map to your headers. You confirm with sample rows before anything is generated.

  • Review the competitive set

    See the homes a buyer would genuinely cross-shop — custom-fit to the subject property, not city-wide averages.

  • Run the pricing story

    Live price × odds × supply: odds of selling at each price, days-on-market pressure, and the inventory a buyer cross-shops.

  • Print or send the flipbook

    One click produces the seller leave-behind and listing flipbook — the same story, formatted for the fridge or your binder.

Fair questions, honest answers

Can I upload my MLS export instead of using a live feed?

Yes — that's the whole point. ListLogic does not connect to any live MLS feed. You export comps from your MLS, upload the file, and ListLogic maps the columns. No vendor approval, no data contract.

How do I create a CMA from a Matrix export?

In Matrix, run your comp search and export to CSV or TXT (the exact menu label varies by MLS). Upload that file to ListLogic, confirm the column mapping, and generate. ListLogic builds the competitive set and price × odds × supply story from your own data.

Does ListLogic work with Flexmls, Bright, and RESO-style files?

Yes. ListLogic accepts common CSV/TXT exports and maps synonyms for the headers you'd see in Flexmls, Bright, and RESO-style files. You confirm the mapping with sample rows before generating, so you stay in control of what each column means.

What file formats can I upload?

CSV and TXT exports are the standard. If your MLS only exports to Excel (.xlsx), save the sheet as CSV and upload that. Matrix, RESO-style, and everyday headers are mapped; you confirm the mapping before generating.

Is ListLogic a live MLS feed?

No. ListLogic is not a live MLS feed and does not connect to your MLS's data service. The data path is Search on any market or Upload your own export — which is why it works with any MLS without vendor approval.

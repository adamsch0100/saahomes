Listings are won with proof, not charm.

When it's hard to sell, sellers already have a number — from a portal, a neighbor, the last agent who promised more. Charm books the meeting. The agent who can show what happens if they stretch the price is the agent they can defend after you leave.

The 3 moments that win listings

Win listing appointments in three places — not with a thicker brochure. A listing presentation that wins is the one the seller can still defend after you walk out.

Moment 1

The pricing conversation

A recommended price is an opinion. Interactive price × odds × supply is a trade-off they can see: stretch the number and competing supply rises while modeled odds drop. You do not have to say “overpriced.” They can see the league change.

Moment 2

Condition adjustments

Two similar cards are not twins. Walk kitchen, garage, lot, and finish out loud so a renovated sold is not treated as proof you are talking them down. Price and condition are the same appointment — the competitive set has to show it.

Moment 3

The leave-behind

You win the listing in the group chat too. Print it or send the flipbook — the same story you just walked, not a different branded PDF with a frozen number. The spouse and the friend who “knows real estate” reopen the field, not just your opinion.

Illustrative — not a measured result

Same week, two appointments

One agent leaves a 20-page solds packet and a promised number. The other walks the curve, names the renovated sold that is not comparable, and leaves the flipbook. The seller hires the proof, not the page count.

Changelog

We ship. Then we ship again.

What's new, what's fixed, and what's next in ListLogic — updated as we build. Found something we should improve? Tell us in the app.

August 25, 2026

New Three levers, providing value, Edit mode

  • Core Facts of ListLogic now include the three levers (price, condition, location) and who is providing the value — listed under yours vs listed over yours.
  • The Market Fingerprint walks pending and sold against price and condition, then shows listed-under (yours is providing the value) and listed-over (they are providing value to yours).
  • Logged-in Live Story: Edit on the far right highlights titles and copy. Click the actual text, click away to save. Restore ListLogic wording from the agent menu. Sample/demo stays read-only.
  • Print Listing Presentation is the name of the one-click landscape print (no longer “leave-behind”).
August 24, 2026

Improved Weekly Fingerprint story + Search refresh

  • The Fingerprint now opens with the similar-set filters (beds, baths, garage, size), the initial list facts, and this week’s note from the listing agent — with earlier weeks in a dropdown.
  • Walk the weeks uses the same compact photo lanes as Where they are now, not big tiles. Tap a week, then a photo.
  • Over/under language is the initial list price, not “the lock.” The public demo is one Greeley listing: the appointment and the weekly Fingerprint, with listing photos pulled for the story.
  • Search Fingerprints refresh from the live market when the API key is on the server. Upload Fingerprints still wait for this week’s MLS export.
August 20, 2026

Improved Fingerprint clocks, comps, and weekly counts

  • The board uses since generate until the home lists, then switches to since active — set the date in the agent console, or we pick it up when that address goes Active.
  • Listed, under contract, and sold are tracked both since listed and since last week’s refresh. Pending and under contract are the same bucket. “Day 0” is gone.
  • Where they are now shows price, beds, baths, and square feet, sortable by those fields. Hover a pin on Where you sit to preview the home and light it up in that set.
August 2026

New Market Fingerprint

  • Every Generate opens a Market Fingerprint: Live Story, print, flipbook, plus a living seller link.
  • Stable seller URL at create. Agent console on the Fingerprint page — lock, contact, hide/revoke, weekly email, refresh or upload, mark sold.
  • Search Fingerprints auto-refresh weekly (data, not email). Weekly email stays opt-in and never auto-starts. Upload stays an explicit export.
  • Then vs now, new over/under the lock, pending/sold from day zero, price rank, photos, and a listing drawer. Talk tracks are rule-based — never a new dollar rec.
August 2026

New Portal chip + weekly pulse

  • Optional Zillow estimate chip on the Live Story — it does not set the recommended list.
  • Lock a list and see new similar homes over and under that line on the report.
  • Opt-in weekly pulse email to you, the seller, or both — listing cards plus a talk track. Never auto-started on Generate.
August 2026

New Comparison pages live

  • New pages comparing ListLogic to Cloud CMA, Saleswise, RPR, and Moxi Present — honest side-by-sides for the listing appointment.
  • New guide: why static CMAs lose appointments when sellers are anchored on Zillow.
August 2026

Improved Feedback loop

  • Send feedback from anywhere in the app — we read every message and ship fixes.
  • This changelog gets updated as fixes land, so you can see your feedback moving.

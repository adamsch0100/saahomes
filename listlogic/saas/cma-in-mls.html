How to create a CMA in Matrix

Skip the in-Matrix CMA builder. Export the comps you already pulled, then upload. Menu labels vary by MLS — use whatever export your board actually offers.

Step 1 — Export

Run your Matrix comp search, then export

In Matrix, run the comp search you'd use for a CMA. Export to CSV or TXT. If the only option is Excel, save the sheet as CSV. You are not uploading photos or documents to the MLS — you are taking a file out.

Step 2 — Upload

Confirm the map, then generate

Upload the Matrix export to ListLogic. Confirm how Beds, Sq Ft, MLS #, and Close Price map to your headers (sample rows stay on screen). Generate the price × odds × supply story. No file? Search any market instead.

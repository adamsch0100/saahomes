How to choose (the 2026 checklist)

1 · Can it build a custom-fit competitive set?

Averages hide the homes a buyer actually cross-shops. The tool should build a competitive set for the specific home — size, beds, garage, age, condition, area.

2 · Can you show the pricing trade-off?

When a seller is anchored on Zillow, comps don't move them. Showing what happens to their odds at every stretch price does. That's the difference between a report and a decision tool.

3 · Does it work with YOUR MLS?

You should be able to use your own data — search any market or upload any export. If a tool forces one pipeline, you're paying for their integration, not your workflow.

4 · Does the seller keep it after you leave?

The pricing story should travel: print it, send it as a flipbook. The listing is won after you leave too.

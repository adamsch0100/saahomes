The problem

Overpricing costs more than most agents — and most sellers — ever see.

The seller already knows what it’s worth: they saw it online. Agents and sellers both underweight how hard that anchor hits odds, timeline, and net — until someone makes the trade-off visible.

01

Sellers anchor on Zillow

A guess with a nice UI beats a spreadsheet of comps. You need a story that's just as easy to believe — but grounded in real data.

02

CMAs don't show trade-offs

Static PDFs can't answer "what if we list $15k higher?" Sellers nod along, then push for the number anyway.

03

Overpricing kills momentum

Two price reductions later, the listing is stale and the seller wonders what went wrong. The data was there — it just wasn't visible.

Why it works

Pricing that's factual because the market is custom-fit.

City-wide stats don't sell a home. ListLogic narrows the lens to the homes a buyer would actually compare against yours — then shows what that market is really doing.

A market built around the home

Size, garage, area, age, and timeframe are filtered to the homes a buyer would genuinely cross-shop — an apples-to-apples segment, not a zip-code average.

How that market is performing

Absorption pace, months of inventory, and buyer demand for this exact segment — a microscope view of whether sellers or buyers hold the leverage right now.

Positioned to sell

A recommended list price and range anchored to what buyers just paid — with the timing and odds trade-offs of pricing higher or lower made visible.

What a seller leave-behind should actually do

A leave-behind only matters if it survives the seven days after your appointment — when the seller is talking to two other agents.

  • Recap the pricing decision, not the tool

    The seller left the appointment with a price range. The flipbook shows why — the cross-shop set and the odds curve, not a list of comps they have to interpret.

  • Show the trade-off at every price point

    Price × odds × supply on one page: what happens to the odds of a sale if they stretch. The argument you made in the kitchen, replayable.

  • Stay readable on phone and binder

    The live link works in the appointment on your screen. The flipbook pages turn on their phone after. The print PDF holds up in a stack of competing agents' folders.

  • Carry your branding, not a vendor's

    Set your name, brokerage, and colors once. Every flipbook, print, and interactive story carries it.

  • Hold up when the seller shows it to the other agent

    If a competing agent made a higher, sunnier claim, the flipbook is the counter: supply pressure and odds, not opinion.

Listing flipbook FAQ

Straight answers on the leave-behind surface.

What is a listing flipbook?

A listing flipbook is a page-turn digital version of a listing presentation the seller can flip through after the appointment. In ListLogic, the flipbook is generated from the pricing story itself — the competitive set, price × odds × supply, and supply pressure — so the leave-behind is the seller's actual market, not a generic agent brochure.

Can I also print the flipbook as a PDF?

Yes. The same story renders three ways: a live interactive link, a print-ready page-structured format, and the flipbook for the seller to page through after. One generation, three surfaces.

What goes into the seller leave-behind?

The pricing decision: the homes a buyer would cross-shop, odds at different list prices, days-on-market and supply pressure, and the market context behind the recommendation — branded to you.

Is this the same as a digital real-estate property brochure?

No. Generic flipbook tools turn a static PDF into a page-turn file. ListLogic generates the pricing story first — live market, custom-fit comps, price × odds × supply — then gives you the flipbook as an output.

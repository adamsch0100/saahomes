What "works with any MLS" actually means here

Not a marketing phrase. Six concrete things, each one visible in the product.

  • No live MLS feed required

    The data path is Search + Upload — not a connection to your MLS's data service. That's the whole reason there's no vendor contract.

  • Search any market nationwide

    Enter an address, set price/beds/baths/sqft filters, optionally draw a map boundary. ListLogic builds the competitive set for that home — no file needed.

  • Upload any MLS export

    Matrix, Flexmls, Bright, RESO-style files, or everyday headers (Beds, Sq Ft, MLS #, Close Price). You confirm the column mapping before generating.

  • No vendor approval, no data contract

    You pay ListLogic directly and keep your MLS. Nothing to get signed off by your board or brokerage.

  • One standard across every market

    Multi-MLS and multi-market agents run the same presentation everywhere — no re-learning a different tool per board.

  • Works without an MLS at all

    The Search path needs no MLS membership, so newer or portal-only agents can still run a full pricing story.

Fair questions, honest answers

What does "works with any MLS" actually mean?

It means ListLogic does not depend on any single MLS. You can search a market nationwide, or upload an MLS export as CSV/TXT (Matrix, Flexmls, Bright, RESO-style). Because the data path is Search + Upload — not a live MLS feed — ListLogic works the same no matter which MLS you belong to, or even if you don't belong to one.

Is ListLogic a live MLS feed?

No. ListLogic is not a live MLS feed and does not connect to any MLS data service. The data path is Search on any market nationwide, or Upload your own MLS export. That is exactly why it works with any MLS without vendor approval or a data contract.

Can I use ListLogic if I don't have MLS access?

Yes. The Search path needs no MLS membership at all — enter the address, set filters, and ListLogic builds the competitive set and market cut from nationwide data. If you do have MLS access, you can also Upload your export. Either way, no live feed is required.

How does ListLogic work with Matrix, Flexmls, Bright, and RESO-style files?

Run your comp search in the system you use and export to CSV or TXT (the menu label varies by MLS). Upload the file to ListLogic, confirm the column mapping — Beds, Sq Ft, MLS #, Close Price — with sample rows, and generate. ListLogic builds the price × odds × supply story from your own data.

Does MLS-agnostic mean I have to leave my current CMA tools?

No. If your MLS bundles a CMA tool, keep it for what it does well. ListLogic is complementary — it's the pricing-decision layer (price × odds × supply) you can run from any market or any export, and it follows you if you change brokerages or markets.

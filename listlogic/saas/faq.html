FAQ

Straight answers for listing agents.

What ListLogic does in the appointment, what your sellers walk away with, and how to get started.

What is ListLogic?

ListLogic builds a microscope view of how your home's market is actually performing — a factual, data-backed pricing story on a market customized to that specific home, not the whole city. Sellers see the competitive picture, a recommended list price and range, and clear trade-offs if they go higher or lower — so you can position the home to sell successfully.

Who is it for?

Listing agents and teams who want a professional, data-backed story at the listing appointment. Built first for Northern Colorado — works nationwide when you search a market or upload a comparable export for the subject home.

Do I search the market or upload an MLS file?

Both. Search builds a market from location, price/beds/baths/sqft filters, and an optional map draw — nationwide. Upload takes a CSV or TXT from any MLS; ListLogic maps the column headers and asks you to confirm them before generating.

How should I filter my MLS search before I export?

Create your seller's custom market — the homes a buyer will actually cross-shop against theirs. In Matrix (or your MLS):

  • Statuses: Active, Pending / Backup, Sold, Expired, Withdrawn
  • Property type that matches the subject
  • Area a buyer would cross-shop
  • Last 2 years (list date or close date)
  • Square footage, beds, baths, garage spaces, and lot size in a band around the subject

Export CSV or TXT (Save As CSV if you only get Excel). Aim for about 500 homes.

After you upload, ListLogic auto-matches columns (MLS #, price, sold price, beds, baths, sq ft, garage, dates, lot, and more). You can change any dropdown that looks wrong, then confirm mapping and Generate.

Will my MLS export headers work?

Yes for common Matrix, RESO-style, and everyday synonyms (Beds, Sq Ft, MLS #, Close Price, garage, lot size, and more). After upload you always review the mapping — with sample rows — and can reassign any field before the report runs.

What does a presentation show the seller?

How their specific market segment is performing — absorption pace, inventory, and buyer demand for homes like theirs — plus a recommended list price and range anchored to what buyers just paid. Live what-if sliders show how timing and odds move with price, so the conversation stays on facts, not opinions.

What does the seller walk away with?

A clear recommended list and range, the competition picture, expected timing and odds at different price strategies, plus the Market Fingerprint: Live Story, a print leave-behind, a listing flipbook, and a living seller link that stays current after they list.

Does Zillow set the recommended list price?

No. A portal estimate can appear as an optional chip on the Live Story so you can talk about the number the seller already saw. It never drives the recommended list, the range, the odds, or the supply math. You choose whether the seller sees it.

What is a Market Fingerprint?

The whole generated package is a Market Fingerprint. Live Story, print, and flipbook are the appointment formats. After Generate, the seller also gets a stable link to a living market picture — what’s on the market now, what was there at Generate, what went pending or sold, new listings over and under the locked list, and price rank. The agent manages list price, seller contact, the link, weekly email, refresh or upload, and mark-sold on that page.

Search Fingerprints refresh weekly whether or not email is on. Upload Fingerprints cannot invent MLS rows — the agent uploads a fresh export. Weekly email is opt-in and never starts automatically when you Generate. No SMS. Talk tracks are rule-based (rank, status, supply) — never a new dollar recommendation, never a portal AVM.

What is the weekly pulse?

The weekly pulse is now the living Market Fingerprint. Search reports refresh the market picture every week. You can optionally email that picture to yourself, the seller, or both. Email never starts on Generate. Upload reports show a loud as-of date until you upload this week’s MLS export.

Can I change the recommended price?

Yes. You stay in control. Adjust the condition/presentation rating, override list price and range, edit advantages and watch-outs, and refine which comps to highlight — then regenerate the packet.

How is the market customized to each home?

Instead of city-wide averages, ListLogic filters to the homes a buyer would genuinely cross-shop against the subject — size, garage, area, age, and timeframe — so every number reflects this home's competitive set. It's an apples-to-apples segment, which is what makes the pricing factual.

How do you measure inventory and demand?

Months of inventory uses Active listings only — homes a buyer can still buy — divided by recent sales pace. Homes already spoken for are counted separately, so sellers see true buyer demand without inflated inventory numbers.

Does ListLogic replace my CMA judgment?

No. It organizes the market story so sellers can see what you already know. Your expertise still chooses the comps, the condition call, and the final list recommendation — ListLogic makes the trade-offs visible and presentation-ready.

What's interactive vs. the print leave-behind vs. the listing flipbook?

Interactive (Live Story) — live on-screen story with charts, what-if pricing, and agent tools.

Print leave-behind — the Live Story printed landscape, one section per page with every chart and graphic. One click from the presentation; print it or save as PDF for email.

Listing flipbook — landscape slides designed for listing presentations and binder printouts.

Is my market data kept private?

The data behind each presentation is used to build your presentation for that subject and market cut. We don't sell it or publish seller addresses as marketing content. Use the same care you already use with client data.

How is ListLogic different from a seller CMA report?

A traditional seller CMA shows comparable sales and maybe a suggested list price. ListLogic adds the trade-off layer: for every possible list price, the odds of selling within a realistic window, against true active supply. The seller sees why the recommended range makes sense — and what happens if they stretch it. The result is a pricing conversation, not a report handoff. Yes — specifically at the pricing conversation, where listings are won and lost. When a seller is anchored on a portal estimate, a static CMA feels defensive. ListLogic turns the appointment into a collaborative pricing decision: the seller sees their own competitive set, the odds at each price, and the supply pressure — so overpricing gets discussed while you're in the room, not after a 30-day stale listing.

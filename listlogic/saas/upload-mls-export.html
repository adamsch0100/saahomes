Fair questions, honest answers

Can I upload my MLS export to ListLogic?

Yes. Upload your MLS export — CSV or TXT from Matrix, Flexmls, Bright, or any RESO-style file — and ListLogic maps the columns. You confirm the mapping with sample rows before generating. No live MLS feed, no vendor approval.

Is this an MLS CSV upload tool, or do I upload photos to the MLS?

This is an MLS CSV upload tool for real-estate comps — you upload your MLS export into ListLogic. It is not a media tool for uploading photos, videos, or documents to the MLS. Import MLS CSV/TXT here; listing media stays in your MLS.

How do I create a CMA from an MLS export?

Create your seller's custom market — the homes a buyer will cross-shop against theirs. In Matrix or your MLS, filter last 2 years; matching property type and area; square footage, beds, baths, garage, and lot size around the subject; Active, Pending/Backup, Sold, Expired, and Withdrawn. Export about 500 homes as CSV or TXT (Save As CSV if you only get Excel). Upload, confirm or correct the auto-matched columns, then generate.

What file formats does the MLS export upload accept?

CSV and TXT are the standard. Matrix, Flexmls, Bright, and RESO-style headers are mapped, plus everyday names (Beds, Sq Ft, MLS #, Close Price). If your MLS only exports to Excel (.xlsx), save as CSV and upload that. You confirm the mapping before generating.

Is ListLogic a live MLS feed?

No. ListLogic is not a live MLS feed and does not connect to any MLS data service. The data path is Search any market nationwide, or upload any MLS export. That is why it works with any MLS without a vendor contract.

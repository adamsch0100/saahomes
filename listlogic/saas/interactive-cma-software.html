What interactive CMA software should actually do

If the tool only makes comp reports clickable, it's not interactive CMA software — it's a clickable CMA. Here's what wins listings.

1 · Live what-if

Show the trade-off, not just the comps

Sellers don't argue with their own eyes. When they see odds drop from 78% to 34% at a stretch price — live, in the appointment — the pricing conversation stops being opinion and starts being math.

2 · Custom-fit competitive set

Built for this home, not the city

City-wide averages hide what a buyer actually cross-shops. Interactive CMA software should build the competitive set from size, beds, baths, garage, age, area, and timeframe — then run the pricing story on that segment.

3 · Works with any MLS

Search or upload — no lock-in

If the tool requires a specific MLS integration, you're not buying software — you're buying their data contract. True interactive CMA software should search any market or accept any CSV/TXT export.

4 · Print + flipbook leave-behind

The story travels after you leave

The interactive appointment is where the listing is won. But the print leave-behind and flipbook are what the seller shows their spouse — and what keeps you top-of-mind against the next agent.

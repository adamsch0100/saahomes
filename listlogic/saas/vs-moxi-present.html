Fair questions, honest answers

Does Moxi Present have live MLS data that ListLogic doesn't?

Moxi is typically integrated with a brokerage's MLS data pipeline. ListLogic takes a different route: Search any market or upload any MLS export — which means we aren't limited to one MLS or one data vendor. Different data architecture, same goal: turn market data into a pricing conversation.

Is this just another CMA?

No. A CMA answers "what did similar homes sell for?" ListLogic answers "what should this home be priced at, and what happens to my odds at every other price?" That second question is what listing appointments actually turn on.

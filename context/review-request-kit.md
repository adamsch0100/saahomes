## What to Ask Clients to Mention (for SEO)

When sending the link, you can say: "If you could mention [city/neighborhood] and what you liked about working with us, that would be amazing!"

**Keywords to naturally include in reviews:**
- Fort Collins, Loveland, Windsor, Greeley, [their city]
- First-time homebuyer / down payment assistance / CHFA
- Responsive / knowledgeable / local expertise
- smooth process / stress-free

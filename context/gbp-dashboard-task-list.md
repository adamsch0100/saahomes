# GBP Dashboard Fixes — Do These Today

These are 5-minute fixes in your Google Business Profile dashboard that directly impact how often you show up in the map pack.

## 1. Products Section (5 min, high impact)
**What:** Kittle lists 10+ "Products" on their GBP: New Construction, Equestrian Homes, Luxury Homes, Condos, Waterfront, Golf Course Homes, Country Homes, Buy a Home, etc.
**Why:** Each product acts like a category trigger. Someone searching "Fort Collins luxury homes" sees Kittle because they have it listed. We don't.
**How:** GBP dashboard → "Products" tab → Add each one:
- Homes for Sale
- New Construction Homes
- Luxury Homes
- Condos & Townhomes
- First-Time Homebuyer Programs
- CHFA Down Payment Assistance
- Veterans Home Buying
- Seller Representation

## 2. Fix Description (2 min, high impact)
**Current:** "Schwartz and Associates is a top real estate team in Northern Colorado working alongside the amazing company and team at Kittle Real Estate."
**Problem:** Free advertising for your #1 competitor.
**New:** "Schwartz and Associates is a top Northern Colorado real estate team led by Adam and Mandi Schwartz. Specializing in first-time homebuyers, CHFA down payment assistance, and helping families buy and sell homes across Fort Collins, Loveland, Windsor, and Greeley."

## 3. Connect LinkedIn (2 min, medium impact)
**Why:** Kittle and Basnar both have LinkedIn linked. Google uses connected profiles as trust signals.
**How:** GBP dashboard → "Info" → "Social profiles" → Add LinkedIn URL.

## 4. Add Attributes (5 min, medium impact)
**What:** Check your GBP dashboard → "Info" → "Attributes" section. Add any that apply:
- Veteran-owned ✅
- Online appointments ✅
- Free consultation ✅
- Offers e-transfer ✅
- Wheelchair accessible ✅

## Core Larimer markets

- **Fort Collins** — Colorado State University, Old Town, Horsetooth Reservoir,
  Poudre Trail, and a strong craft-food-and-brew scene. GreatSchools top marks for
  Fossil Ridge and Liberty Common. See the full neighborhood breakdown from
  [Fort Collins real estate agents](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/fort-collins/).
- **Loveland** — The Sweetheart City sits between Fort Collins and Denver with
  Benson Sculpture Garden, Boyd Lake State Park, and a growing downtown arts
  district. See [Loveland CO homes for sale](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/loveland/).
- **Estes Park** — Rocky Mountain National Park gateway with cabins, condos, and
  vacation-home appeal. See [Estes Park CO real estate agents](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/estes-park/).
- **Red Feather Lakes** — alpine lakes and cabin living in northwest Larimer
  County, 45 minutes from Fort Collins. See
  [Red Feather Lakes cabins for sale](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/red-feather-lakes/).
- **Bellvue** — Poudre Canyon foothills and acreage minutes from Fort Collins. See
  [Bellvue CO acreage for sale](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/bellvue/).

## Weld County growth corridor

- **Windsor** — Weld RE-4 schools, Windsor Lake, Water Valley, RainDance; one of
  the region's strongest family markets. See
  [Windsor CO homes for sale](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/windsor/).
- **Greeley** — Weld County's largest city with the University of Northern
  Colorado, historic downtown, and the region's most accessible entry prices. See
  [Greeley real estate agent](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/greeley/).
- **Timnath** — master-planned new construction on the Larimer-Weld line with
  Fort Collins proximity. See
  [Timnath CO homes for sale](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/timnath/).
- **Johnstown** — historic main street plus I-25/US-34 crossroads growth. See
  [Johnstown CO homes for sale](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/johnstown/).
- **Berthoud** — small-town charm between Loveland and Longmont, I-25 accessible.
  See [Berthoud Colorado real estate agents](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/berthoud/).
- **Carbon Valley** — Firestone, Frederick, and Dacono: affordable new
  construction along I-25. See
  [Carbon Valley CO homes for sale](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/carbon-valley/).
- **Fort Lupton** — US-85 corridor value between Denver and Greeley. See
  [Fort Lupton CO homes for sale](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/fort-lupton/).

## Boulder County edge

- **Erie** — one of the Front Range's fastest-growing towns on the

- **Brighton** — I-76 value with Adams County schools and Barr Lake recreation.

- **Lyons** — Boulder County foothills at the North and South St. Vrain

  [Lyons CO homes for sale](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/lyons/).

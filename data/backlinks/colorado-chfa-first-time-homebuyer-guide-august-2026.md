# Colorado CHFA & First-Time Homebuyer Guide — Northern Colorado (2026)

A practical, verified overview of Colorado's CHFA down payment assistance programs and
related homebuyer resources for Northern Colorado — compiled from the SAA Homes
(Schwartz and Associates, Coldwell Banker Realty) program guides on saahomes.com.

## What CHFA offers

The Colorado Housing and Finance Authority (CHFA) is the state's affordable-homeownership
partner, offering fixed-rate first mortgages paired with down payment assistance through a
statewide network of participating lenders. Assistance comes as either a **grant of up to
$25,000 or 3%** of the first mortgage (no repayment), or a **deferred second mortgage of up
to $25,000 or 4%** (no monthly payments; repaid only when you pay off, sell, or refinance).
A minimum $1,000 borrower contribution and a homebuyer education class are typically required.

Explore the full breakdown of [Colorado down payment assistance programs](https://saahomes.com/chfa-down-payment-assistance/).

## Common program requirements

- Credit score: minimum mid-score of 620 (some programs allow no credit score)
- Borrower contribution: at least $1,000 toward the home purchase
- Homebuyer education: complete a CHFA-approved class before closing
- Income & purchase price limits vary by county, household size, and program

## Programs relevant to Northern Colorado buyers

- **CHFA SmartStep / Preferred / FirstStep** — first mortgage programs paired with grants or
  deferred second mortgages for general, conventional, and first-time buyers.
- **CHFA Schools To Home** — up to **25% down payment assistance** for full-time Colorado
  public school employees. See the [CHFA Schools To Home](https://saahomes.com/chfa-schools-to-home/)
  guide for educators.
- **Colorado Champions Home Loan** — for first responders and their households, raising CHFA
  income eligibility to **110%** of standard caps (created by SB26-053, launch expected
  late 2026). See the [Colorado Champions first responder program](https://saahomes.com/colorado-champions-home-loan-program/).
- **Greeley G-HOPE** — up to **$8,000** forgivable down payment loan by zone for full-time
  employees of Greeley-area employers. See the [Greeley G-HOPE program](https://saahomes.com/greeley-g-hope-down-payment-assistance/).

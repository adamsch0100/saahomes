# Northern Colorado Boulder County & Carbon Valley Guide — August 2026

A town-by-town look at the Boulder County and Carbon Valley markets of Northern
Colorado — compiled from the SAA Homes (Schwartz and Associates, Coldwell Banker
Realty) area guides on saahomes.com. These communities sit along and just east of
the I-25 / US-287 corridor between Boulder and Greeley, and together cover the
full spectrum of Northern Colorado value from premium foothills living to
first-time-buyer entry points.

## Longmont
Longmont is the largest city in Boulder County outside Boulder itself — a
tech-and-agriculture town with a historic Main Street downtown, the St. Vrain
Greenway trail network, and Union Reservoir recreation. Buyers choose it for
Boulder County access at prices that sit below the county seat.
Browse [Longmont CO homes for sale](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/longmont/).

## Boulder
Boulder is an iconic Colorado city at the foot of the Flatirons, anchored by CU
Boulder and a premium lifestyle that draws buyers from across the country. It is
the highest-priced market in the SAA Homes service area, with distinct character
from Chautauqua and University Hill to North and South Boulder.
See [Boulder Colorado real estate](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/boulder/).

## Firestone
Firestone is a cornerstone of the Carbon Valley — a rapidly expanding Weld County
area along the I-25 corridor north of Denver. Families choose it for newer homes,
strong schools, and relative affordability compared to Boulder County, with
commuter access to Longmont and Fort Collins.
Explore [Firestone CO new construction homes](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/firestone/).

## Frederick
Frederick offers some of the most accessible home prices in the Carbon Valley
while still delivering new construction, community amenities, and St. Vrain
Valley schools. Located between Firestone and Dacono, it continues to draw buyers
priced out of Boulder and Larimer counties.
See [Frederick CO affordable homes](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/frederick/).

## Evans
Evans is a growing Weld County community adjacent to Greeley — offering
affordable home prices and suburban living with easy access to UNC, medical
centers, and regional employers along the US-34 corridor.
Browse [Evans CO homes for sale](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/evans/).

## Niwot
Niwot is one of Boulder County's most desirable small communities — known for its
historic Old Town, excellent schools, and Left Hand Valley setting between
Longmont and Boulder. Buyers choose it for walkable charm and strong property
values.
See [Niwot CO real estate](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/niwot/).

## Mead
Mead is a quiet Weld County town between Longmont and Fort Collins — offering
peaceful country living, open-space views, and easy I-25 access for commuters who
want more home and land for their budget.
Explore [Mead CO homes for sale](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/mead/).

## Milliken
Milliken offers affordable small-town living in Weld County, positioned between
Greeley, Loveland, and Johnstown for easy commuting. Its central location and
competitive price points make it a strong fit for first-time buyers.
Browse [Milliken CO real estate](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/milliken/).

## Severance
Severance has transformed from a quiet agricultural town into one of Northern
Colorado's fastest-growing communities — with newer homes, larger lots, and a
blend of rural character and suburban convenience between Windsor and Greeley.
See [Severance CO new construction homes](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/severance/).

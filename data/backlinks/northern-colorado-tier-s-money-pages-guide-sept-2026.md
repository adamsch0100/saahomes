# Fort Collins, Loveland, Windsor & Greeley — Northern Colorado Tier S Home Search Guide (September 2026)

A market-by-market look at the four highest-demand Northern Colorado cities —
compiled from the SAA Homes (Schwartz and Associates, Coldwell Banker Realty)
area guides on saahomes.com. These four anchor Larimer and Weld county buyer
and seller demand along the I-25 and U.S. 34 corridors.

## Fort Collins
Fort Collins is Northern Colorado's premier market — the Larimer County seat,
home to Colorado State University, and a perennial top-ranked place to live.
Buyers choose between Old Town historic homes, Midtown established
neighborhoods, and newer construction in south and east Fort Collins, while
sellers benefit from steady demand across Poudre School District catchments
and the city's strong job base.
Work with [Fort Collins real estate agents](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/fort-collins/) who know every neighborhood.

## Loveland
Loveland — the Sweetheart City — sits between Fort Collins and Denver along the
I-25 corridor, anchored by a thriving arts scene and lakefront recreation on
Boyd Lake. Buyers compare historic downtown homes, lake and golf communities,
and new construction to the north and east, with mountain views throughout.
Search [Loveland CO homes for sale](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/loveland/).

## Windsor
Windsor is one of Northern Colorado's most sought-after family communities,
between Fort Collins and Greeley, with top-rated Weld RE-4 schools and extensive
trails. Its position on the Weld–Larimer county line gives buyers a meaningful
choice between county tax bases and school footprints.
Explore [Windsor CO homes for sale](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/windsor/).

## Greeley
Greeley is the largest city in Weld County and Northern Colorado's most
affordable major market, anchored by the University of Northern Colorado.
West Greeley near the Poudre River Trail, the University District, and the
growing south and east sides each offer distinct value — and the city's G-HOPE
program adds forgivable down payment loans for qualifying employees.
Connect with [Greeley CO real estate agents](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/greeley/).

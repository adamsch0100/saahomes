# Northern Colorado City Market Guide — August 2026

A city-by-city snapshot of the Northern Colorado housing market, compiled from the
live IRES MLS data published by SAA Homes (Schwartz and Associates, Coldwell Banker
Realty). Figures reflect the current area guides on saahomes.com as of mid-August 2026.

## Fort Collins
The average home price in Fort Collins is approximately $636,000 as of mid-2026, driven
by Colorado State University, tech and healthcare employment, and steady in-migration.
First-time buyers may qualify for CHFA grants up to $25,000 through SmartStep or FirstStep.
Browse current [Fort Collins homes for sale](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/fort-collins/).

## Loveland
Loveland's July 2026 median sale price was approximately $510,000, up 3.6% year-over-year,
with single-family homes generally ranging from $450,000 to $650,000. Inventory has grown
roughly 14% versus July 2025, giving buyers more negotiating room.
Explore [Loveland CO real estate](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/loveland/).

## Windsor
Windsor's July 2026 median sale price was approximately $588,000 (up 2.1% year-over-year),
the highest median among Northern Colorado's four major markets. Homes in the $450K-$600K
range typically sell within 25-35 days when priced correctly.
See live [Windsor CO homes for sale](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/windsor/).

## Greeley
Greeley offers Northern Colorado's most affordable major market, with single-family homes
typically ranging from $375,000 to $500,000. The city's G-HOPE program adds $2,500-$8,000
in zone-based forgivable down payment assistance for qualifying employees.
Work with a local [Greeley real estate agent](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/greeley/).

## Timnath
Timnath is dominated by new construction, with homes typically ranging from $550,000 to
$850,000+ in master-planned communities like Timnath Ranch and The Farm. New homes carry
HOA fees of roughly $50-$150/month.
See [Timnath CO homes for sale](https://saahomes.com/northern-colorado-areas/timnath/).

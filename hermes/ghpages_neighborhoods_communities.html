Best Neighborhoods & Communities in Northern Colorado (2026)

Choosing a neighborhood in Northern Colorado usually comes down to schools, commute, and what the budget can reach. This town-by-town guide points you to local area pages from Schwartz and Associates (SAA Homes), Coldwell Banker Realty — serving Larimer, Weld, and Boulder counties.

Larimer County core neighborhoods

  • Fort Collins neighborhoods — Old Town, CSU-area condos, and east-side and south-side master plans.
  • Loveland CO neighborhoods — mature east-side blocks, downtown, and newer west-side build-out.
  • Windsor CO neighborhoods — Water Valley, RainDance, and established lakefront and golf communities.

Weld County communities

  • Greeley CO neighborhoods — west-side growth, UNC-adjacent blocks, and established central neighborhoods.
  • Fort Lupton CO neighborhoods — value communities along the US-85 corridor.
  • Johnstown CO neighborhoods — historic Main Street and new phases at I-25 / US-34.

I-25 & Boulder-county communities

  • Timnath CO neighborhoods — master-planned communities on the Larimer–Weld line.
  • Erie CO neighborhoods — St. Vrain Valley communities for Denver and Boulder commuters.
  • Carbon Valley CO neighborhoods — Firestone, Frederick, and Dacono family communities.
  • Brighton CO neighborhoods — Denver-metro communities along I-76.
  • Berthoud CO neighborhoods — small-town blocks between Loveland and Longmont.

Foothills & mountain communities

  • Estes Park CO neighborhoods — mountain communities at the gateway to Rocky Mountain National Park.
  • Red Feather Lakes CO neighborhoods — cabin and acreage communities north of the Poudre.
  • Lyons CO neighborhoods — Boulder County foothills along the St. Vrain River.
  • Bellvue CO neighborhoods — Poudre Canyon communities minutes from Fort Collins.

The best way to narrow a neighborhood search is to pair a local area page with a conversation about your commute, your school priorities, and your budget. Start with the SAA Homes buyer resource hub.
